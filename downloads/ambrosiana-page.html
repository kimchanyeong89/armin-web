Browse the online collection catalogue of the Pinacoteca to access a wide selection of works and detailed information.
The images available through this search engine are intended exclusively for study and consultation purposes. Saving or downloading is not permitted. The Ambrosiana is willing to grant reproduction rights for images relating to its manuscript and printed book collections, archival materials, engravings, drawings (including Leonardo’s Codex Atlanticus), as well as the Pinacoteca’s collections, including statues, paintings, and materials identified by inventory numbers. The procedures for obtaining authorization vary based on the type of use requested. For information or to submit a request, please consult this page
